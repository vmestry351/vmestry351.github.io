Credit risk and loan default analysis dashboard

Independent project in SQL and Power BI.

Goal
Find what drives loan defaults and give underwriters clear recommendations.
What I did
Audited and cleaned 32,581 loan records in MySQL, removing 165 duplicates and invalid values and imputing missing interest rates and employment lengths, leaving a validated 32,409-row dataset. Wrote SQL queries to find default drivers and built a High/Medium/Low risk segmentation.
Takeaway
Default rate rose from 9.96% (Grade A) to 98.44% (Grade G), and from 11.6% to 70.4% across debt-to-income brackets. The risk segments split default rates 62.95% vs 5.9%. I delivered an insights report with underwriting recommendations and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
